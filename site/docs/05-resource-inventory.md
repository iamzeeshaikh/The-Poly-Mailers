## Purpose and boundary

Each resource states the capability or product page it must not duplicate.

### Poly Mailer Size Reference

* **Purpose:** A lookup table: find your item, read across, get a size.
* **Nearest internal page:** `/size-guide/`
* **Boundary:** This is the reference table. The size guide is the decision framework for working it out yourself.

### Comparing Poly Mailer Materials

* **Purpose:** How to compare films between suppliers when the published numbers do not match.
* **Nearest internal page:** `/materials/`
* **Boundary:** The materials page states what we supply. This guide teaches comparison across any supplier.

### Preparing Artwork for Mailer Printing

* **Purpose:** A start-to-finish walkthrough for someone preparing packaging artwork for the first time.
* **Nearest internal page:** `/artwork-guidelines/`
* **Boundary:** This guide teaches the process. The artwork guidelines page is the specification to hand a production designer.

### Selecting a Mailer Colour

* **Purpose:** Choosing a colour that still looks right after a supply chain has handled it.
* **Nearest internal page:** `/poly-mailer-colors/`
* **Boundary:** The colour collection routes you to a product. This guide is about the decision itself and how colour ages.

### Closure and Self-Seal Reference

* **Purpose:** How closures work, why they fail, and which construction suits which operation.
* **Nearest internal page:** `/self-seal-poly-mailers/`
* **Boundary:** The product page sells the closure. This reference diagnoses why one failed.

### Protective Mailer Comparison

* **Purpose:** A lookup table from contents to construction, and an honest list of what mailers cannot do.
* **Nearest internal page:** `/protective-mailers/`
* **Boundary:** The collection routes you to products. This reference is the lookup table behind the decision.

### Sustainable Packaging Terminology

* **Purpose:** What each term commits you to, and how to test a supplier claim against it.
* **Nearest internal page:** `/sustainable-poly-mailers/`
* **Boundary:** The collection helps you pick a product. This guide defines the language before you make any claim about it.

### Ordering Mailers Internationally

* **Purpose:** What actually differs between the four markets, and what to settle before ordering.
* **Nearest internal page:** `/shipping-and-delivery/`
* **Boundary:** The shipping page describes our process. This guide is written from the buyer’s side of the transaction.

### Custom Mailer Ordering Checklist

* **Purpose:** Eleven decisions to settle before you ask anyone for a price.
* **Nearest internal page:** `/how-it-works/`
* **Boundary:** The how-it-works page describes our process. This checklist is what you do before contacting anyone.

### How to Measure for a Mailer

* **Purpose:** The three measurements that matter, and how to take them on an awkward product.
* **Nearest internal page:** `/resources/poly-mailer-size-guide/`
* **Boundary:** The size reference is a lookup table. This guide is how to get the numbers to look up.
